FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about reaching the team, using the contact channels, and understanding how enquiries are handled.

You can reach the team through four primary channels: the official phone number (tap-to-call), the official email address, the contact form on this page, or through verified social channels. Each route is suited to different needs — urgent matters may be best handled by phone, while detailed enquiries work well through email or the form.

The official email address is displayed on the Email contact card and in the quick contact strip at the top of this page. When you tap or click it, your default email application will open with the address already filled in. If the email address has not yet been published, a placeholder will appear — please check back or use the contact form instead.

Tap the Call contact card or the phone number in the quick contact strip. On mobile devices, this will open your phone's dialler with the number pre-filled. On desktop, it will trigger whatever calling application your system has registered. If the number has not yet been published, a placeholder will appear.

Official updates are published on this website and through verified official social channels. Any social channel that has been officially confirmed is marked with an "OFFICIAL" badge on this page. Channels without that badge are shared but not yet confirmed as official accounts.

Use the contact form on this page. Fill in your name, email, subject, topic and message, then click Send Message. Your email application will open with your message prepared — no information is stored on this page, and nothing is transmitted until you send the email yourself.

For community concerns, use the contact form and select "Community issue" as the topic. This helps the team route your message appropriately. For urgent community matters, the phone channel may be more suitable — please use whichever is most appropriate for your situation.

Media enquiries — including interview requests, document requests and information requests — can be submitted through the contact form using the "Media" topic. Please include your publication or organisation, the nature of the request, and any relevant deadlines so the team can respond appropriately.

Organisations interested in partnerships can use the contact form with the "Partnerships" topic. Please include details about your organisation, the nature of the proposed collaboration, and how it aligns with the constituency's development priorities as outlined in the Vision and Manifesto pages.
